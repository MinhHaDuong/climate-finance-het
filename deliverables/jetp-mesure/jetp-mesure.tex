\begin{abstract}
This package freezes a reproducible preliminary result, displays its central
figure, and specifies the next manuscript and re-harvesting iteration. The
underlying status is \JetpDataStatus. It deliberately does not develop polished
article prose: the decision needed now is whether the observed documentary
asymmetry is a useful constraint on a substantive JETP question, and which
records must be recovered to answer that question.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Question and provisional contribution}
The eventual paper asks: which functions of a just transition become concrete
within JETPs, with what public, private, or mixed finance, and from which
pre-existing operation histories? This is a non-causal question. A reported
stage is not speed, and a post-agreement milestone is not an effect of the
agreement.

The frozen corpus gives a narrower preliminary result: its three ingredients
are documented at very different assertion-level coverage and cannot yet be
joined for the same atomic assertions. That is a finding about the present,
bounded documentary corpus, not a finding that JETPs lack functions, finance,
or histories. It does not establish acceleration or additionality; it does not establish private-finance mobilisation. The sought substantive result remains
the operation-level relationship among transition function, finance, and
history after targeted re-harvesting and reconciliation.

\section*{Key frozen results}
The 0730 snapshot contains 1,740 atomic assertions: 1,729 positions, 7 events,
and 4 coverage assertions. It retains unknowns rather than converting them to
zero or dropping them. No currency conversion, ownership inference,
operation-level matching, or causal estimation enters the counts.

Only four of the 303 instrument observations state ownership literally (one
public, two private, one mixed/unallocated); the remaining 299 cannot be
classified from a funder name alone. There are 297 reported monetary positions,
but their currencies, scopes, financial objects, and potential duplicates do
not support a portfolio total. South Africa contributes 257 register-status
labels, not a transition clock; Vietnam contributes seven event assertions and
three named incompatible reconciliations, not a national finance total.

\section*{Iteration and re-harvesting agenda}
The next iteration is not a cascade restart. Preserve every current atomic
assertion, then add rather than replace evidence: (i) harvest operation-level
agreement, implementation, and retirement records for the named Vietnam cases;
(ii) recover financial-object, currency, ownership, and allocation fields for
the 303 instrument assertions; (iii) link only source-supported duplicates and
retain conflicts as intervals or incompatible alternatives; and (iv) extract
social objectives, beneficiaries, and realised outcomes separately. Re-run the
same three-panel descriptive output after each increment, while publishing both
coverage and the linked subset denominator.

\section*{Traceability and limits}
The frozen inputs are \texttt{docs/jetp-study/0730-descriptives.json}, its run
manifest, and the 0823 figure manifest; their SHA-256 values are recorded in
the latter. The figure can be regenerated with
\texttt{python3 scripts/jetp/build\_0823\_figure.py --root .}. This package
does not infer missing values, treat source publication dates as event dates,
or evaluate realised justice outcomes. Its coverage result is provisional; the
sought substantive result requires new linked records.
