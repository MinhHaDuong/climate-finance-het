# Citation coverage over time (note for codebook.md, data/products/, ed04 kit)

Displaced from data-paper.qmd §3 by cut-plan v2 item W3 (ticket-ref 0274).

Citation coverage — the share of corpus works that appear as a citing source
in the citation network — is unevenly distributed over time: approximately 27%
for works published before 2007 versus 47% for post-2015 works. This gap
reflects two compounding factors: early and grey-literature works are less
likely to carry DOIs or to have their reference lists indexed by Crossref and
OpenAlex, and the field itself was smaller before 2007. Users conducting
temporal citation-network analyses should account for this systematic
undercount of early-period connectivity.
