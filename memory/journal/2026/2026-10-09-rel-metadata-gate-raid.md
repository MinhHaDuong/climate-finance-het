# REL metadata gate raid

An exploratory session on the OpenAI Decisions endpoint led to a raid over the REL ingesters, the venue score, the dedup and the abstract recovery. PRs 1719, 1721-1726, 1727, 1728-1731, 1733-1752 landed on main; tickets 2040-2043, 2045-2052 were filed (2044 closed as a tracker superseded by 2047 and 2048, 2040, 2041, 2042, 2045, 2046, 2047, 2049, 2050 and 2052 closed). Open at the end: 2043 (profile, switched off), 2048 (dedup rule, stage B2), 2051 (deferred).

**Measured.** The rebuilt pool matched the 2026-10-01 baseline (389,291 works from 426,636 records). Abstracts were missing for 157,312 works (40.4%; 148,437 with a DOI). Probes on abstract-less works with a DOI: Crossref 2 of 400, Europe PMC 7 of 400, ISTEX 341 of 600; the full ISTEX run accepted 81,051 of 148,437 (54.6%), raising abstract coverage from 59.6% to 80.4% and the first act from 40.1% to 75.4%. Resource types: 30,726 of 32,744 blank values recovered from OpenAlex for USD 0.0317. The OpenAlex backfill cost USD 0.303 for 302,926 ids (authors on 97-98%). Semantic Scholar with the renewed key: single-DOI requests hit HTTP 429 at any pace tried; two batch requests of 100 gave 2 abstracts for the ICF and unsure residue and 13 for works ISTEX supplied. The regenerated lane deliveries kept every record and work_key (0 non-blank cells overwritten) and changed 3,570 first_author values through lane order. A first count of "365 works with two or more DOIs" was a counting error (340 hold one SICI DOI containing a semicolon; the true count is 25). The dedup rule panels: working-paper rule precision 0.975 on 400 pairs (readers Claude, GPT-6.1-sol, Mistral); OpenAlex-duplicate rule 0.900 on 300 pairs (Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek), 0.995 where first authors agree.

**Events.** OpenAlex rejected a 200-id filter batch (HTTP error) and accepted 100. The OpenAI account returned 429 insufficient_quota during a panel. A coder's `pkill -f` killed its own background run. The first deliveries stopped on a truncated DOI that matched 3,669 ISTEX records. Padme was shut down for a GPU upgrade with stage B2a not started; its brief is on ticket 2048.

**Decisions of the author (2026-10-09).** The earlier ingesters were a first try and may be redone; the metadata profile requires a venue identity (journal, series or publisher by any route), not a publisher string, and keeps resource type required; a platform name in the journal field is not a venue; five platforms (ceew, cpd, ersa, south_centre, unfccc) are accepted identifier sources; tier C stays 0 except a tier-C venue listed in a trusted index scores 0.5 (2,008 works); ISTEX abstract texts are for internal use (provisional); a working paper and its published article are one work, window -1 to +5 years, an explicit lane link overrides it, the published article names the work; panels up to USD 10 with Claude, Gemini and DeepSeek ratified as readers; the OpenAlex-duplicate rule goes on with a first-author guard; label migration (stage B2) starts in scratch.

**Review attribution not captured.** The raid ran about 25 read-only review seats (sonnet and opus through the Agent tool, one narrow re-review each at several PRs, 1721-1752). The trail in the session names the pinned model token and the findings, but not the verbatim provider-qualified model id, effort or model-version; no attribution records were written.
