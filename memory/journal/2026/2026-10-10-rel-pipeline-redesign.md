**Decisions of the author (2026-10-10).** Pipeline: N upstream databases, N lanes, pool of records, dedup, pool of works, filter on DataCite metadata completeness, scoring, alpha-cut at most 10,000 works. Scoring: two experts from two vendors (Haiku and Luna), each with its own calibrated cut, never averaged; a work is divergent when the two fall on opposite sides of their cuts; a local arbiter with llguidance and positive and negative few-shots on divergent works only; its non-English reading is to be measured. The Gavard set is verified, sealed and calibrates the cut; the author hand-picks a sealed test set of 50 positives and 50 non-obvious negatives; few-shots come from a separate set. The journal summaries lane t1650 is turned off. The staged screening half of 1654 is retired. 1654 is split into 2061, 2062, 2060 and 2063. A recommendation not yet decided: withdraw t1650 in the next pool build and finish the round-one merge before the dedup version-2 flip (2048 B2b).
