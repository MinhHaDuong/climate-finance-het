## From pipeline to financing

Indonesia's official progress report separates approved programmes and projects, grants, and a further financing pipeline. Direct lender and operator pages corroborate parts of this record. Technical assistance, policy lending and investment projects have different purposes and should be compared separately.

## Reading dated observations

Later project pages sometimes report a more advanced stage than the annual report. The timeline retains both dated observations rather than rewriting history. Capacity differences and suspended projects remain visible in the notes; they are not silently resolved by choosing the most optimistic account.
