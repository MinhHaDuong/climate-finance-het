## An investment plan becoming projects

Senegal's investment plan combines quick wins, submitted proposals, programmes and components. Project identities have been reconciled where the documents support the match. Needs and prospective allocations describe the proposed financing design, not funds already paid.

## Preparation is visible, totals remain bounded

Agency, operator and funder sources expose studies, procurement and some later milestones. Their connection to the original plan is sometimes provisional. Programme and component budgets must not be added together. The source-linked record is stronger on the preparation process than on aggregate disbursement.

## Latest documentary follow-up

The September 13 follow-up directly corroborates 21 of the 43 identities; 19 remain supported only by the central plan and three ASER routes remain blocked. These are evidence-coverage counts, not implementation rates. ANER's July 2025 strategic plan confirms the university, hospital and public-institution solar-autonomy proposal. Its FCFA 60.4 billion conditional budget is a financing need, not a signed agreement or an additional amount to add to the plan.

The new loan and procurement documents also sharpen the limits of attribution. BOAD's approval for the single Linguère solar-storage plant does not confirm continuity with the Champions Nationaux ownership vehicle. Rural contract and advance-payment-order records do not establish bank settlement or an exact crosswalk to all proposed village programmes. These distinctions remain attached to the individual project sources and observations.
