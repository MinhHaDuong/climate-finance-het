## Financing and institutional change

South Africa has the most extensive public register in this collection. Its reported allocations include policy lending, grants and investment finance. The official quarterly report gives a clearer aggregate picture than a sum of project entries, which can describe different funding windows and stages.

## What the register cannot establish

A register date is not automatically the date a loan was signed. Implementation and completion labels in programme records do not establish payments or commissioned assets. Read the financing observations together with their individual source notes.
