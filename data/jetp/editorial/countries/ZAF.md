## Reporting baseline: Q1 2026

This view uses the JET IP Quarterly Progress Report for 31 March 2026, published on 26 June 2026. The official report index and resource hub were checked on 14 September 2026; no Q2 2026 edition was found on those routes. Collection in September does not make the reported outcomes a September snapshot.

## Financing at two levels

The Q1 report records USD 6.12 billion allocated across financing instruments against USD 14.36 billion pledged (42.6%). Its project/portfolio register covers USD 4.32 billion of allocations. The report explains that instrument-level allocations include financing not yet decomposed into registered project rows; these totals have different coverage. Neither figure measures disbursement. See the linked headline report, section 3 and Tables 3–4 (printed pages 12–13).

## Report totals and register reconciliation

Table 5 (printed page 14) reports 257 records: 23 planned, 18 approved, 129 in implementation and 87 completed. Our archived register also contains 257 rows, but its status labels yield 128 in implementation and 88 completed, with planned and approved counts unchanged. The difference is unresolved; the report totals are retained as attributed observations and do not overwrite individual register records.

The catalogue has 263 South African identities: the 257 register rows plus six separately documented project/programme records. Those six are outside the imported register and are not an increase in the Q1 report's project count. The financing-evidence chart describes our coding, while Table 5 describes reported implementation status.

## What the register cannot establish

A register date is not automatically the date a loan was signed. Implementation and completion labels in programme records do not establish payments or commissioned assets. Read financing observations together with their source notes.
