# Mechanism aliases — version 2026-10-08

Recognise translations, accents, spelling variants and abbreviations in the record's language; this list provides systematic category coverage, not a finite claim to every possible synonym. An alias is identification evidence, not automatic membership: verify its meaning and the same main studied object, climate purpose and financial instrument/institution relationship.

- Clean Development Mechanism: CDM, MDL, Mécanisme de développement propre, Mecanismo de Desarrollo Limpio, 清洁发展机制; certified emission reductions/CERs/URCEs/减排量 when associated with CDM.
- Joint Implementation: JI, mise en œuvre conjointe/MOC, aplicación conjunta, совместное осуществление; emission reduction units/ERUs/UREs. Kyoto Protocol Article6 refers to JI; distinguish it from Paris Agreement Article6.
- Paris Agreement Article6 cooperative implementation: Article6.2, internationally transferred mitigation outcomes/ITMOs; Article6.4 mechanism, Paris Agreement Crediting Mechanism/PACM, older retrieval alias Sustainable Development Mechanism/SDM (not a separate current formal mechanism); Article6.8 non-market approaches require an actual finance/institution object rather than cooperation wording alone.
- Green Climate Fund: GCF, Fonds vert pour le climat/FVC, Fondo Verde para el Clima/FVC, 绿色气候基金.
- Global Environment Facility: GEF, Fonds pour l'environnement mondial/FEM, Fondo para el Medio Ambiente Mundial/FMAM, 全球环境基金. Climate purpose must be established: GEF biodiversity or unrelated environmental finance alone does not establish climate membership.
- Adaptation Fund: AF, Fonds pour l'adaptation/FA, Fondo de Adaptación, 适应基金. Identify the fund rather than a generic use of “AF”.
- Least Developed Countries Fund: LDCF, Fonds pour les pays les moins avancés/FPMA, Fondo para los Países Menos Adelantados. Special Climate Change Fund: SCCF, Fonds spécial pour les changements climatiques/FSCC, Fondo Especial para el Cambio Climático. Other named international climate funds include Climate Investment Funds/CIFs, Clean Technology Fund/CTF, Strategic Climate Fund/SCF (SCF can also mean Standing Committee on Finance; resolve context).
- International forest/climate finance: reducing emissions from deforestation and forest degradation/REDD/REDD+, réduction des émissions liées à la déforestation et à la dégradation des forêts, reducción de emisiones por deforestación y degradación forestal; results-based payments/results-based finance/RBP/RBF/paiements fondés sur les résultats. REDD+ or forest conservation alone does not establish an international finance relationship.
- Climate development finance: climate ODA/official development assistance/APD climatique/ayuda oficial al desarrollo climática, multilateral development bank/MDB/BMD climate lending, World Bank/Banque mondiale/Banco Mundial and regional banks' climate finance, bilateral donor climate finance, Rio markers/marqueurs de Rio/marcadores de Río, climate-aid accounting, grant equivalents, additionality, USD100billion goal, New Collective Quantified Goal on Climate Finance/NCQG (retrieval variant: New Quantitative Collective Goal)/nouvel objectif collectif quantifié sur le financement climatique/NOCQ, nuevo objetivo colectivo cuantificado sobre financiamiento climático, Just Energy Transition Partnership(s)/JETP, partenariat pour une transition énergétique juste, and their country implementations; Fast-Start Finance/FSF, Copenhagen/Cancun USD100billion commitment and post2025 climate-finance goals. Actor names alone are not climate purpose.
- Loss and damage finance: loss-and-damage fund, Fund for responding to Loss and Damage/FRLD, fonds pour les pertes et préjudices, fondo para responder a las pérdidas y los daños. Loss/damage impacts without finance or its institutions are distinct.
- International private climate-finance mobilisation: cross-border private investment for explicit climate purposes, concessional or blended climate finance, climate-finance risk guarantees, international green/climate bonds, debt-for-climate/debt-for-nature swaps with explicit climate purpose. Generic ESG/green bonds without an international relationship and explicit climate purpose do not establish all facets.

Additional international forest-finance and risk-finance families (official source review 2026-10-08):
- Amazon Fund/Fundo Amazônia/Fundo Amazonia: internationally donor-funded results-based emissions-reduction finance, despite Brazilian administration; named fund identification supplies mechanism context, not a match on the geographic word Amazon. Official donor/emissions evidence https://www.fundoamazonia.gov.br/pt/transparencia/doacoes/ ; https://www.fundoamazonia.gov.br/en/transparency/donations/ .
- Forest Carbon Partnership Facility/FCPF, its Readiness Fund and Carbon Fund: multilateral REDD+ support and verified emission-reduction payment contracts; resolve ambiguous “Carbon Fund” to FCPF rather than assume identity. https://www.forestcarbonpartnership.org/carbon-fund .
- BioCarbon Fund/BioCF, Initiative for Sustainable Forest Landscapes/ISFL: donor-supported multilateral greenhouse-gas finance. https://www.worldbank.org/content/dam/Worldbank/document/SDN/BioCF_ISFL_Flyer.pdf .
- Norway's International Climate and Forest Initiative/NICFI: bilateral climate/forest finance and results-based payments. https://www.nicfi.no/about-us/how-do-we-work/ . UN-REDD Programme/UNREDD: donor-funded programme, but a technical conservation object alone does not establish finance. https://www.un-redd.org/post/norway-announces-nok-100-million-new-support-un-redd .
- InsuResilience Global Partnership and Global Shield against Climate Risks; Global Shield Financing Facility/GSFF: international climate-risk finance/insurance arrangements. https://www.insuresilience.org/updated-governance-charter_2020-2-16-1/ ; https://www.globalshield.org/ ; https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2022/11/14/world-bank-group-launches-global-shield-financing-facility-to-help-developing-countries-adapt-to-climate-change .
- African Risk Capacity/ARC/ARC Ltd and Caribbean Catastrophe Risk Insurance Facility/CCRIF/CCRIF SPC: international risk-pooling institutions with mixed hazards; ARC also covers epidemics and CCRIF earthquakes, so the record must establish climate-relevant risk rather than automatic climate membership. https://www.arc.int/about ; https://www.ccrif.org/about-us?language_content_entity=en .
