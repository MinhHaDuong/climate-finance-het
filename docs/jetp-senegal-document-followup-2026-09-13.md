# Senegal document-led follow-up, 13 September 2026

## Result

Targeted retrieval of named documents resolves one more identity: **Annex 23,
ANER's solar-autonomy programme for universities, hospitals and public
institutions**. Coverage advances from the managed-round checkpoint of
20 collected /20 central-only /3 blocked to **21 collected /19 central-only
/3 blocked**. Twenty-two of the 43 identities still lack direct corroboration.
No financial or implementation event is added in this follow-up.

Three teams pursued contract, village-schedule and original-project records.
Solar used 12 web calls; rural used 13, exceeding its initial 12-call limit by one
final identifier check; ANER used 11 and followed two additional PDF identifiers
from the official catalogue. Parent verification covered charging attachments,
OMVG study references and contact provenance. This was document retrieval with
specific identifiers, not another claim of exhaustive web coverage.

## ANER: an original strategic plan changes the identity verdict

ANER's [official document catalogue](https://www.aner.sn/api/documents), record5,
exposed the [final PSD2025–2029](https://www.aner.sn/uploads/plan_strategique_developpement_aner_2025_2029.pdf).
The cover says July 2025. Pages 22 and 48 name the university, hospital and public-
institution solar-autonomy project. The latter gives a **FCFA 60.4 billion
conditional budget**. The distinctive beneficiary classes, purpose and ANER
promoter faithfully restate Annex 23; the budget is also consistent with the
investment plan's rounded EUR 92 million need. Independent review accepts a
confirmed project-source link.

The PSD separately lists the Casamance health programme. Its narrower
192-facility scope therefore remains separate. The 100 public-building PV
minicentrals and 60 MWc on p.21 are expected results of Objective 2.1; they are
neither delivered capacity nor a site schedule assigned to Annex 23. A
conditional strategic budget is not a funding agreement. The ledger's existing
need observation remains; no second financial row is created.

The API also exposed the [final report of a preliminary rooftop survey](https://www.aner.sn/uploads/rapport_final_rooftop_solar_senegal.pdf),
record 9. It identifies MPE as beneficiary, World Bank study funding and
GOPA/Akilee as consultants. Printed pp.12–13 distinguish a 25-building shortlist
from a narrower eligible set; the design scope is roughly 1 MWc. This is a useful
possible predecessor, not an exact join to the broader programme. The
[WP2 productive-use study](https://www.aner.sn/uploads/wp2_upe_giz_senegal_final.pdf),
record 3, discusses the Gandiaye desalination pilot with insufficient financial
information. It supplies no original 200-unit desalination or TURBULENT fiche.

Catalogue dates use first-of-month values and database creation dates. These
are retained as provenance, without inventing exact publication days. The PSD
registry date stays blank with July 2025 in its notes; the rooftop report's date
also remains unresolved.

## Rural contracts: precise references, still no village crosswalk

[ARCOP decision138/2024](https://arcop.sn/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/DECISION-N%C2%B0138-2024-du-24-ARCOP-CRD-DEF-du-24-DEC-2024.pdf),
p.3, describes an ASER–AEE Power EPC package for 928 localities, a
FCFA 91,833,980,000 pre-tax contract, a 25 November 2023 tripartite engagement and
a 21 December 2023 subcontract. Those references make the next request much more
specific. They do not establish the 600-village/24 MWc/injection proposal or
supply its village annex.

[Decision107/2024](https://arcop.sn/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/DECISION-107-2024-ARCOP-DEF-DU-02-OCTOBRE-2024.pdf)
records a Santander letter and temporary suspension in October 2024. Its advance
figure and the euro figure later recited by decision 138 differ and are not
reconciled automatically. These historical records are not independent bank-
settlement evidence or proof of a continuing suspension in 2026. The December
disposition closes conciliation and refers the parties to courts; allegations
are not treated as established criminal guilt. Annexes 5–7 remain blocked.

Both PDFs were retrieved with ordinary curl after the harvester received
HTTP 406. The failed and successful observations are preserved with actual
response timestamps and hashes. Parent review checked the original pages as
well as the OCR.

## Solar, charging and pumped storage: requests now name the missing pieces

BOAD's [2024 Annex5 signed-instrument list](https://admin.boad.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/A05_LISTE-DES-AP-PP-ET-CP-SIGNES-2024.pdf)
shows the disclosure needed for Linguère: operation number, signature date,
counterparties and financing terms. It is a template from another year, not
proof of a 2026 agreement. No current signed-instrument list or executed
Linguère agreement surfaced. The CNTIC contract covers, Koungheul safeguard or
land record and Champions Nationaux legal-vehicle crosswalk also remain missing.

The charging notice 107072743 lists five attachments: solicitation and amendment
on 15 April 2026, and clarification files on 24 April and 8 May. Its exact public
[download-list endpoint](https://afd.dgmarket.com/tenders/biddingDocumentsList.do?noticeId=107072743)
returned HTTP 403. The request names those files and asks for the TOR, any award
and an explicit Annex 35 link. It does not treat the unread files as evidence.
Senelec's [AGPM2026](https://www.senelec.sn/media/marches/documents/AVIS_GENERAL_-_2026_VF.pdf),
p.4, verifies its procurement mailbox.

For Sambangalou STEP, no institutional EU TAF/OMVG study report or workshop
record was retrieved. A continental pumped-storage screening study is a
different exercise. The request asks OMVG to confirm the professional-account
references to the 3 June 2026 workshop, identify the study contract and provide a
public report. The conventional 128 MW dam remains separate.

## Six reviewable institutional requests

The [request packet](jetp-senegal-document-requests/README.md) contains French
messages for SENELEC, BOAD, ASER, ANER, OMVG and GFA, each as Markdown and EML,
plus a CSV status register. The SENELEC solar and charging questions are
consolidated; no CC/BCC or mass-copy list is used. General mailboxes explicitly
serve as routing contacts. ASER and ANER contact addresses were verified in
their public website code, which is archived because their HTML pages are
client-rendered shells.

An independent request review corrected the CNTIC chronology, the distinction
between a final report and its preliminary survey, the ANER procurement
identifier's origin in a mirror URL, and GFA's contact-source URL. All messages
remain **draft_not_sent**, with empty sending dates. No institution has been
contacted in this research pass.

The original fiches, contract covers and site schedules can resolve remaining
joins. Absence of a response or of a public download would remain an access
observation, not proof of abandonment, funding or delivery.
