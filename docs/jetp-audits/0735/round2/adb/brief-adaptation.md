Before external searches, 2026-09-14; Execute phase under ticket 0735.

Round 1 did not establish a verified World Bank milestone pair. This independent
route tests ADB institutional catalogues, project data sheets (PDS), and completion
reports for all-sector lifecycle information and historical population boundaries.
Country strata: Indonesia and Viet Nam; Philippines and Bangladesh as the first
two non-JETP borrowers, India as an additional coverage check. Seek water/sanitation
and transport alongside energy and social sectors. These choices reflect ADB
coverage and documentary diversity, never observed duration.

Accept identity only with the same official project/operation identifier and,
where events are loan-specific, the same loan identifier. Approval/signature and
signature/effectiveness qualify only when labelled actual and linked to that loan.
Planned dates, publication dates, revised closing dates and disbursement totals do
not substitute for actual events. Parent/additional financing remain separate.

population, including proposed, active, closed and cancelled operations, while PDS

needed: catalogue schema/export and concrete PDS/completion records across country
and sector strata. Stop at discriminating evidence or 30 query/URL units / 60 min;
count failures and individual URLs. Report missing/censored cases and distinguish
current status from archival entry/removal. No shared-ledger edits or claims of
construction, causal effects, or certified donor controls.
