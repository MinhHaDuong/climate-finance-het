## Population boundary and contrary evidence

EIB's [pipeline explanatory notes](https://www.eib.org/en/projects/pipelines/notice/index), sections on inclusion, stage and updates, establish that public projects and non-exempt private projects enter before Board examination, at an appraisal-dependent time. Confidentiality can exclude projects. Signature moves projects to the financed list, with only six further months in the pipeline list. Only location, sector and status automatically update. Thus present pipeline membership cannot be treated as past membership or complete inception coverage. The inspected notes do not establish archival retention of dropped proposals or a revision log. [Financed-project list](https://www.eib.org/en/projects/loans/index.htm) explicitly covers signed financing contracts; it excludes never-signed candidates by construction.

An unsigned comparison case is EIB **20180765**, Alexandria Abu Qir urban rail, Egypt: page status Approved, **20 September 2023**, release **31 January 2023**; no signature shown. It is a child of an urban transport framework, so a parent signature must not be substituted. “Approved” is the page's current recorded status, not independent proof that no contract exists elsewhere. [Official project, summary and Milestone](https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20180765).

Historical reach differs by field: EIB **19942037**, ASECNA Senegal transport, exposes **20 April 1995** signature, but no approval pair in the inspected record. Long signature history therefore does not establish equally long preparation histories. [Official Signature(s)](https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/19942037).

AFD [transparency page](https://www.afd.fr/en/transparency-and-dialogue), Inform section, reports 81% coverage at end-2021 of financing above EUR100,000 within its transparency scope. This is historical, qualified coverage, not a present completeness estimate. It links its own open-data and IATI disclosures. Proposed/declined/cancelled inclusion and historical revisions were not established. No ordinary IATI transaction was interpreted as approval.
