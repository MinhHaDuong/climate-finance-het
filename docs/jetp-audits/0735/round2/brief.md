# Round 2: broader institutional and country evidence audit

Frozen before external research, 14 September 2026. User direction: another pass,
not restricted to World Bank, energy, or JETP countries. Parent ticket 0735 feeds
0729. Round 1 established two WB rosters but no verified approval/signature pair;
it did not map the wider institutional opportunity. This round changes lenders,
catalogues and sector/country coverage, not just keywords on failed routes.

## Acquisition scope

Three independent source scouts:

1. **ADB**: official projects/loan databases, operational programmes, project data
   sheets, legal agreements and evaluations. Start with Indonesia/Viet Nam plus
   Philippines, Bangladesh and India; all-sector catalogue first, then energy,
   water/sanitation, transport and social infrastructure examples as available.
2. **AfDB**: official project portal, appraisal/implementation reports, country
   indicative operational programmes and evaluations. Senegal/South Africa plus
   Kenya, Morocco and Ghana are acquisition strata; cover energy and at least
   two non-energy sectors where documents exist.
3. **EIB and AFD**: official project/finance databases, appraisal and signature
   records, open-data/IATI disclosures and evaluations. Inspect coverage across
   JETP and other borrowing countries, including Morocco/Tunisia/Egypt and other
   documented borrowers. Test both institutions; give priority to the one with
   usable histories rather than force equal numbers.

Countries are source-discovery strata, not a matched donor pool or certified
untreated controls. Record historical eligibility/income and exposure gaps rather
than restrict to a single income class before knowing source coverage. No country
or project is selected because its apparent speed supports a preferred effect.

## First documentary acceptance check

For each institution, test whether an official record distinguishes at least two
actual lifecycle milestones on the same operation/loan. A page timestamp, planned
approval, loan signing, effectiveness and first payment must remain distinct.
Also test the available population boundary: proposed/active/closed/dropped and
historical snapshots. A pair found in a completed report does not establish the
population denominator; an all-sector current list is not a historical risk set.
Missing or failed checks must be returned explicitly.

## Required evidence and return

Each scout owns its subdirectory (adb, afdb, eib-afd); no shared ledger edits or
Git mutations. Return a report, source/call log and operation/event evidence table.
Include official URLs, identifiers, exact locators, event vs publication dates,
retrieval dates and original byte hashes only where bytes were collected. A
successful official web extraction without local bytes remains unarchived.

Record which fields are demonstrated rather than merely documented in a schema:
identification/concept, appraisal, approval, signature, effectiveness, procurement,
first disbursement and administrative closure. Physical construction is outside
this short-paper feasibility exercise. Look deliberately for unfinished, dropped
or cancelled cases and for archival entry/removal rules. Keep project, loan,
parent and additional-financing identities distinct. Attempt representative cases
from at least two non-JETP countries and two non-energy sectors per institutional
track; missing coverage is a finding, not a reason to relabel a case.

Evaluate bulk export/API availability, historical reach, definition changes,
revision histories and cost of extending the sample. Financial-flow data with
amounts but no milestone semantics cannot silently replace process dates.
Cross-lender definitions are not assumed comparable. Rank acquisition routes by
usable dates AND population coverage, independently of observed durations.

## Budget and ownership

Up to 30 external query/URL retrieval units per track or 60 minutes, whichever
first (90 units total discovery ceiling). Count batched URLs separately, and
failed attempts. Inspect primary originals; snippets remain leads. Report a
midpoint yield. Stop early if the required discriminating evidence is obtained;
new rounds require an explicit new document/route/question. No institutional
messages, source promotion, effect estimation or causal-design selection.

Parent independently checks pivotal originals and reconciles cross-track results
in a synthesis. That separate verification log has a 15-unit cap; it does not
extend a scout's discovery budget. Reports are candidate research evidence,
with no changes to website counts. First-round reports remain historical records.
