# Third-pass evidence brief

14 September 2026. Author-directed expansion: dig AFD historical coverage;
add JICA, KfW, USAID and UK sources across sectors and borrower countries.
Round 2 acquired 2,080 AFD financing records but did not establish portfolio
completeness or historical retention. New discriminating routes: AFD catalogue
and predecessor/current tables, lender IATI and official project histories.

Acceptance: exact provider/operation/financing identifiers, separate project
hierarchies; source-defined dates and transaction types; distinguish actual from
planned events, reporting windows from event time, missing from zero. Country
location is not JETP attribution. Non-energy is not automatically untreated.

Budgets: AFD parent discovery at most 30 external request/search units;
JICA and KfW each 20, USAID/UK jointly 25; parent cross-provider verification
at most 10 additional units. Log attempts including failures. Stop at budget
or when the next discriminating acquisition can be specified. No saturation
claim, institutional outreach, effect estimation or canonical ledger changes.
The first AFD searches and catalogue probe preceded this written brief; the
question and bounded scout instructions were announced before those calls.

Deliverables: source-linked lender reports, actual-data profiles where acquired,
source manifests/logs, cross-lender acquisition priorities and ticket update.
A current export must fail as evidence of a complete historical population unless
inclusion, retention and temporal membership are separately demonstrated.
