# Allocation mechanisms and conditional design implications

No causal design is selected. “Go” means documentary work can proceed, “narrow”
means the claim must be restricted, and “defer” means identification is not
established. Sources and locators are in [sources.csv](sources.csv).

| Candidate mechanism / design family | Original evidence examined | Missing identification evidence | Conditional implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| G7 country shortlist / matched or synthetic comparison | S01 records a collective decision and named candidates. | Full candidate universe, evaluations/rejections, confounding history, exposure and outcome comparability. | **Go** for descriptive selection chronology; **defer** causal identification. A shortlist does not reveal the assignment process. |
| Country allocation score / threshold discontinuity | S01 gives qualitative rationale and a Senegal exception; no quantitative country score in inspected text. | Original scoring formula, running variable, cutoff, eligible observations, manipulation audit and funding consequence. | **Defer.** No usable country threshold established in this search. This is a scope-limited finding, not proof no rules exist. |
| Externally scheduled staggered rollout / event-time comparison | S01's prospective Senegal COP27 target contrasts with actual June 2023 launch in S07. | A binding schedule fixed independently of expected outcomes; dated anticipation and financing exposure. | **Narrow** event dates to observed milestones; **defer** exogenous rollout claim. Calendar differences alone are insufficient. |
| Political relationships as an instrument | Qualitative commitment and cooperation language; no original rule making a relationship an allocation instrument. | Relevance plus a credible exclusion argument and independence evidence, including other bilateral flows (S11). | **Defer.** Political affiliation is not promoted to an instrument. |
| Indonesian project screening / threshold or priority comparison | S10, the Secretariat's own undated FAQ, describes MEMR/PLN longlist collection, significance to pathway, entity/government commitment, start by 2030 and no financial close when CIPP drafted; subsequent strategic prioritization. | Dated operative CIPP Chapter 5/version; complete accepted/rejected longlists, scores, exceptions, decision dates and actual financing assignments. | **Go** for a narrowly specified next documentary route; **defer** causal use. The 2030 screen is project eligibility, not random support allocation or a defensible discontinuity by itself. |
| Cross-sector comparison / energy versus water | S02/S08 document broader scope; S11 documents parallel natural-resource cooperation. | Sector/operation exposure map, lender reallocation and spillovers, common shocks, outcome compatibility. | **Narrow** any sector definition; **defer** presumed untreated water or other sectors. |

The separate allocation search (unit 14) found the Secretariat's original
screening explanation. It did not retrieve the operative Chapter 5 or a country
allocation rulebook. A funding funnel, priority designation and financial award
must remain separate stages.
