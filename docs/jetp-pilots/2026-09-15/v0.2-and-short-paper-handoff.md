# MVP v0.2 and short-paper handoff: design only

This handoff translates the causal-pilot synthesis into a sequence of decisions.
It intentionally authorises no collection, code, data transformation, website
publication, estimator, or manuscript result.

## MVP v0.2 boundary

MVP v0.2 remains a source knowledge base. Its future acceptance work should
preserve the migration train's contracts: immutable source versions, typed dates
and evidence roles, provenance, candidate isolation, explicit uncertainty and
recoverable releases. Facts remain source-linked and are never reclassified as
additional investment or a JETP effect by analytical timing alone.

If approved after the migration train, a derived-analysis interface may attach a
versioned country-quarter observation snapshot to cited source facts. It must be
clearly labelled as derived, keep missingness/lost-visibility visible, preserve
the distinction among pledges, approvals, signatures, disbursements and delivery,
and remain separate from canonical source records and public country totals.

## Short-paper decision path

```text
Merged lender pilots
        |
        v
0729 comparative country-quarter feasibility screen
        |
  GO -- author approves frozen protocol --> 0730 implementation --> 0732 paper
   |
NARROW / DEFER -- author scope decision --> no causal-effect substitution
```

The 0729 screen is specified in [the synthesis](synthesis.md). It selects neither
a country nor an estimator in advance. Its output is an auditable GO, NARROW or
DEFER with frozen input/version references and the required reasoning.

Only a GO followed by author approval may create a 0730 execution ticket. That
future ticket must pre-register the exact outcome, country-period unit,
operational treatment date, comparison pool, exclusions, treatment alternatives,
missingness rules, uncertainty procedure and falsification plan before effect
estimation. The central figure for 0732 is then the three-panel diagnostic and
effect presentation specified in the synthesis, tied to the frozen analysis.

## Explicit non-actions

- Do not acquire further donor, country, comparator, or historical sources.
- Do not build a panel, calculate a treatment effect, render a result, or infer
  additionality.
- Do not add derived data to canonical JETP records or change the public MVP.
- Do not draft causal findings for the short paper.

These boundaries keep a useful feasibility failure publishable as a methods/data
finding while preventing it from becoming an unsupported JETP-effect claim.
