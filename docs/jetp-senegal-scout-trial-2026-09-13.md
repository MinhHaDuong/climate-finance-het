# Senegal: bounded smaller-model scout trial, 13 September 2026

This report records the initial trial. See the [subsequent managed rounds](jetp-senegal-managed-rounds-2026-09-13.md) for later evidence and adjudication.

Three `gpt-5.6-sol` scouts made 31 web-tool calls across all 24 unresolved
identities: solar/network (7 identities, 11 calls), rural programmes (7, 12)
and other promoters (10, 8). The parent independently read candidate evidence,
checked novelty against 260 registered sources and adjudicated the matches.
This was a bounded feasibility trial, not a controlled model comparison;
it does not measure relative accuracy, cost or speed against a single-agent run.

One identity advances: PUELEC QW4 now has direct corroboration for a delimited
component. Coverage becomes **20 collected, 20 central-only and 3 blocked**
across 43 canonical identities. **23 still lack qualifying direct evidence.**
Ten sources were registered, nine documents archived and one retrieval failed.
There is one new physical observation and no new financial event.

## Accepted evidence

The [ministry report](https://energie-mines.gouv.sn/programme-durgence-delectrification-puelec-medina-kenioto-magnankanti-et-matakoussi-illumines-pour-la-premiere-fois-depuis-lindependance-du-senegal/), published 9 November 2025, names PUELEC,
ASER supervision, Global Engineerings SAR and Senegalese state funding. It
reports commissioning at Medina Kenioto, Magnankanti and Matakoussi during
a two-day tour beginning 6 November. These are three of 39 localities then
being electrified, not evidence that all 39 or all PUELEC were completed.
The ledger date is the report date; exact commissioning days remain unknown.

The ministry reports FCFA 1,429,995,123 including tax for the package.
That claim is retained separately: it establishes neither disbursement nor
JETP/IPG attribution, so it adds nothing to either financial scope.
A project-specific report by the state funder qualifies as direct evidence;
a central investment plan alone does not. The regression test enforces that
boundary and leaves the three separate ASER proposals blocked.

contains exact titles for Annex 28 (PDF p.52, printed p.43, footnote 31) and

evidence: locations, durations and estimated budgets remain leads, with no
financing or implementation event. PDF creation metadata is not a publication
date, and the cover's academic year does not settle chronology.

## Implication for ticket 0720

Small scouts can widen discovery, but this trial does not establish
that a larger swarm would close the remaining gaps. The observed gain was
one accepted identity out of 24 searched, plus leads requiring stronger
identity evidence. The immediate next inputs are ASER submission dossiers
and village lists, Senelec project/contract identifiers and ownership records,
and ANER's original project fiches underlying the thesis. Additional searches
should target those documents rather than repeat broad project-name searches.
Ticket 0720 remains open; search exhaustion is not proof of project abandonment.
