*Noted 2026-09-23, from the donnees-inter-reseaux announcement of 2026-09-17 (Cyril Heude).*

**Paper concerned:** `deliverables/multilayer/multilayer-detection.qmd` —
*Multi-layer Detection and Validation of Structural Change in Text Corpora*.
No target venue has been chosen for it yet.

## The journal

- ***Data & Corpus — La revue des données en SHS***, ISSN 3099-5299, on
  Episciences (open access). Issue 2 is online:
  <https://dc.episciences.org/fr/volumes/1064>, themed on data articles in
  the social sciences and humanities: new societal stakes and methodological
  innovation.
- Accepts three article types: data papers, **methodology articles**, and
  articles on tools/platforms/software for collecting, processing or
  valorising data.
- French or English. 30 000–50 000 characters with spaces (flexible if
  justified).
- Author guidelines: <https://dc.episciences.org/fr/for-authors>;
  submission via <https://manager.episciences.org/dc>; contact
  dc@episciences.org.

## Fit

- **For:** a "methodology article" slot matches a paper whose contribution is
  a detection-and-validation protocol for structural change in a text corpus;
  the SHS-data readership (CNRS inter-network data community) is the
  practitioner audience that would reuse it. Diamond OA on Episciences, no APC
  found.
- **Against:** a very young journal (volume 2), indexing in Scopus / Web of
  Science not verified and unlikely yet; low citation ceiling compared with
  *Scientometrics* or *QSS*. Length cap (≤ 50 000 characters) would force a
  cut of the current draft.
