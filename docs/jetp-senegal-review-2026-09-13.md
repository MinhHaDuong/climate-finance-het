# Senegal JETP — documentary review, 13 September 2026

All 49 plan lines have been reconciled to 43 project/programme identities.
The public-source review now has 20 direct corroborations, 20 `central_only`
verdicts and three `blocked` ASER routes. These are not 43 additive assets or
43 financed projects. The literal requirement for a direct page for every
project remains unmet for 23 identities.

Financial needs, reported funding and physical implementation are separate
observations. Project estimates below are not secured finance. Programmes and
components overlap, notably PUELEC. Missing pages do not establish abandonment
or absence of activity. The plan's precise issue date remains unresolved.

The [deeper case analysis](jetp-senegal-case-analysis-2026-09-13.md) adds
solar site evidence, a rural feasibility-study lead and financial-design analysis.
The 23 unresolved direct identities remain open; new possible matches do not
change the coverage verdicts.

## Findings from the resumed pass

- Diass: operator-reported groundbreaking on 31 March 2026; no commissioning
  observed. The 2026 Senelec notice and earlier AFD factsheet remain separate.
- BioGNV: IFC 609428 identifies PPP advisory support. Its approval does not
  establish capital financing for the plant.
- ASER: both public hostnames refuse connections. Annex 8 nevertheless specifies
  the generic proposal as 330 localities plus 150 repowering localities.
- KASOFOR, CAA 065, Gandiaye, Much Cooler and the Water Compact provide relevant
  context without establishing identity with the submitted JETP proposals.
- A repost reports a June 2026 Sambangalou pumped-storage workshop. The original
  publication/report was not retrieved; the conventional dam remains distinct.
- The allocation table on p.46 reverses QW5/QW6. Amounts are joined by title.
  The 34/38 count and 1000/2000 borehole discrepancies are also preserved.
- Saloum has a EUR30m summary cost and a EUR20m prospective allocation in the
  plan. These are different quantities; neither proves a signed loan.
- The unconfirmed AFD–Senelec EUR 670 million claim remains excluded.
