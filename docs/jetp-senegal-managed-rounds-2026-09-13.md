# Senegal JETP managed research rounds, 13 September 2026

## Scope and status

Three bounded scout streams followed the initial evidence review: solar and storage, rural electrification, and ANER programmes. Solar used 18 web calls in round 2, 10 in round 3, and 6 in round 4. Rural used 18 calls in round 2 and 10 in round 3. ANER programmes used 11 calls in round 2 and 10 in round 3, supplemented by public API and Common Crawl retrievals. The rounds searched for legal and procurement identifiers rather than accepting matches based on similar titles, capacities, or village counts.

The final conservative research outcome is **20 collected, 20 central-only, and 3 blocked**. Annex 35 remains a provisional preparation lead with no implementation event. Financing and physical or preparatory observations remain separate throughout.

## Solar and storage

The strongest new documentary chain concerns the SENELEC solar-storage package at Ouarkhokh in Linguère. The [Energy Ministry's 28 July 2025 notice](https://energie-mines.gouv.sn/partenariat-senelec-et-huawei/) (body paragraphs 2–3) reports that SENELEC and CNTIC signed two EPC+F contracts, in partnership with Huawei, for two 50 MWc plants with storage stated as 30 MW/90 MWh. The wording does not establish Huawei as a signatory and leaves unclear whether the storage figure applies to each plant or to the pair.

A [BOAD-hosted provisional resettlement plan](https://admin.boad.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/PAR_Centrale-Solaire_Ouarkhokh_VP_02022026_Send-Senelec_Huwaei.pdf) (printed p. 18/PDF p. 17) instead says an EPC contract with CNTIC for financing, development, and construction of the exact Ouarkhokh 50 MWc plus 30 MW/90 MWh plant was signed on 24 July 2024. The 2024 and 2025 dates must both be preserved: they may describe different instruments or an amendment, but the original contract covers and numbers have not surfaced.

Physical evidence is strong for the distinct operator package. The [Ministry's first-stone article](https://energie-mines.gouv.sn/pose-de-la-premiere-pierre-de-la-centrale-solaire-photovoltaique-a-linguere-le-senegal-accelere-sa-souverainete-energetique/) was published 10 April 2026, but does not state the ceremony date. The [Genertec Chinese notice](https://www.intlgt.cn/xwzx/gsxw/202605/t20260526_57364.html) (opening paragraphs, published 26 May 2026) says the project entered implementation and calls SENELEC the project owner. The parallel [English Genertec page](https://www.intlgt.cn/en/NewsMedia/Latestnews/202606/t20260616_57438.html) has no visible publication date; its URL date is not publication evidence. The [Ministry's 13 August 2026 notice](https://energie-mines.gouv.sn/visite-de-travail-sur-invitation-du-groupe-huawei-technologies-co-ltd/) (final paragraph; visit began 11 August) ties a later SENELEC-Huawei memorandum to both Linguère and Koungheul. SENELEC's [2026 procurement plan](https://www.senelec.sn/media/marches/documents/PPM_SENELEC_2026_VERSION_2_VALIDE_LE_13_03_2026.pdf) (PDF p. 5, `C_DQSE_273`) also names both sites in planned HSE monitoring.

These shared sites, actors, and aggregate configuration establish a strong possible relationship to QW2, but not its defining Champions Nationaux identity. The available records describe a SENELEC-led package and never identify the proposed wholly Senegalese IPP/SPV, shareholders, PPA, or a legal crosswalk to QW2. Koungheul still lacks its own safeguard, land, contractor, or works document. QW2 therefore remains central-only, with no physical event assigned to it. No external legal record for the separate Fonds Leral/QW3 proposal was found.

The financing result is narrower. BOAD's [150th Board communiqué](https://admin.boad.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/COMPRESS-CA-150-1.pdf), dated 27 March 2026, introduces approved loans on p. 2 and records on p. 3 an approval of **FCFA 41.5 billion** for partial financing of SENELEC's 50 MWc plus 30 MW/90 MWh Linguère plant. This is valuable context for the distinct plant. It does not prove a signed agreement, effectiveness, disbursement, total cost, Koungheul financing, legal borrower capacity, or JETP/IPG attribution, and it does not cure the missing QW2 ownership join.

## Rural electrification

The [ARCOP audit of ASER's 2023 procurement](https://arcop.sn/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/RAPPORT-AGENCE-SENEGALAISE-DELECTRIFICATION-RURALE-ASER-2023-1.pdf) (printed/PDF pp. 55–56, `T_ASER_003`) identifies lots `T0038` and `T0039` awarded to Global Engineering SARL. Their FCFA 867,233,917 and FCFA 562,761,206 values sum exactly to the Ministry's FCFA 1,429,995,123 PUELEC component, with matching agency, contractor, and compatible geography. The audit records signatures on 15 December 2023, approval on 5 January 2024, and start orders on 5 February 2024.

This is a strong inferred procurement join for QW4, but the financial boundary remains important. Page 56 records a transfer order and payment orders `0083/0082` for FCFA 285,999,023 in startup advances. Those are issued payment documents, not proof of bank settlement or recipient confirmation. They also do not establish completion across all villages or JETP/IPG finance. The exact contracts and orders are identified; settlement remains unproved.

For Annex 5, the official [GIZ PED II project record](https://www.giz.de/projektdaten/projects.action?pn=202020998&request_locale=en_GB) identifies project `2020.2099.8`, an EUR 11.6 million programme commitment, and GFA as participant. Search-engine extraction of GIZ's 2024 award list points to a EUR 954,180 GFA consultancy on indexed PDF p. 46, but the [listed GIZ URL](https://www.giz.de/en/downloads/giz2024-de-vertraege-firmen-institutionen.pdf) returned 404 and no original was archived. It is therefore a secondary search lead, not evidence equivalent to the archived official 2025 list, which records a separate EUR 199,340 Speedykom-GFA consultancy. Neither lead names MRV, ASER, 350 villages, or 10 MW. Those amounts cannot be allocated to MRV's first-party study claim. Annexes 6 and 7 also remain blocked: PAMACEL's 330 localities and the reported AEE Power 600-village target are distinct or unjoined programmes, not identity matches.

## ANER programmes and charging preparation

The archived ANER [2025 general procurement notice](https://www.aner.sn/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/AGPM-2025.pdf) (Common Crawl `CC-MAIN-2025-13`, WARC offset 613321930, length 96900) lists a feasibility-study procurement for the public-buildings electrical-autonomy project. It supports the Annex 23 project family and feasibility stage. It does not make the narrower D2B17SN01 operation for 192 health facilities in Casamance coextensive with Annex 23. Likewise, archived ANER pages for ESIP, ESIPC IV, public buildings, and the single Gandiaye desalination pilot are narrower identities whose budgets and outputs cannot be transferred to the broad annex proposals.

The official [Investment Plan Annex 8](https://www.vie-publique.sn/docs/91d17da4-9ebc-472c-bcc2-0192d159188a/annexe-8-analyse-des-projets-plan-dinvestissement-transition-energetique-senegal-enercap.pdf) identifies Annex 28 as received project 25 (report p. 172) and Annex 30 as received project 27 (pp. 58–61). These exact primary evaluation records confirm ANER sponsorship and early maturity, but omit the submitted fiches' locations, unit counts, budgets, durations, and any TURBULENT partner identity.

Annex 35 is a strong but provisional preparation match. SENELEC's procurement plan (PDF p. 13, `C_DEG_155`) seeks a consultant for a national mobility-electrification master plan covering deployment of EV charging infrastructure and names `ACF-DON`. The dgMarket notice `107072743` repeats the same code and body funding attribution, although automated retrieval is blocked and its AFD hosting metadata is misleading. Same promoter, unique procurement code, and exact charging-deployment scope support an inferred component relationship. The plan does not say “public,” cite Annex 35, record an award, show installed chargers, quantify an executed grant, or establish JETP attribution. The adjudicated result is central-only coverage and no physical or preparation event.

## OMVG pumped-storage lead

Parent searches sharpened the Annex 38 lead without establishing the project identity. The dry-search ledger names first-person professional profiles for Roger Gaillard and Pierre-Luc Laflamme and consultant routes involving EU TAF, ISL Ingénierie, and 3E Ing. around a 3 June 2026 Sambangalou pumped-storage pre-feasibility workshop. These are precise people-and-firm discovery leads, but no OMVG or EU TAF workshop record, contract, terms of reference, or institutional study report was retrieved. ISL's [site search for Sambangalou](https://isl.fr/?s=Sambangalou) returned no project content; 3E Ing.'s [planning portfolio](https://3eing.ca/compagnies-electricite/etudes-planification/) describes relevant power-system study capability but does not identify Sambangalou or OMVG.

The [OMVG energy portal](https://www.pe-omvg.org/) and the Senegal water ministry's [works notice](https://mha.gouv.sn/barrage-hydroelectrique-de-sambangalou-la-reprise-imminente-des-travaux/) describe the conventional 128 MW Sambangalou dam and regional interconnection. They cannot be joined to the proposed pumped-storage addition. Annex 38 therefore remains central-only until the EU TAF/OMVG pre-feasibility report, workshop record, or exact study contract becomes public.

## Search learning and exact unlocks

The solar round-3 saturation claim was premature. A broader but bounded `site:boad.org Linguère 50 financement` query found the decisive Board communiqué after narrower project-page searches had failed. Future lender searches should cover Board-session communiqués and signed-agreement annexes before declaring saturation.

The remaining joins require specific documents: executed Linguère and Koungheul EPC/EPC+F covers with numbers, dates, prices, and site allocation; a Koungheul ESIA/PAR, land act, or works notice; a BOAD signed agreement, effectiveness or disbursement notice, and any parallel Koungheul approval; a Chinese lender or Sinosure instrument naming the site or contract; and an SPV registry record, licence, PPA, shareholder schedule, or explicit Champions Nationaux crosswalk. Rural unlocks are bank settlement or recipient evidence for the PUELEC advances, the GFA-MRV subcontract/TOR and 350-village list, and signed AEE Power/CESCE technical documents. ANER unlocks are the submitted fiches for received projects 25 and 27, the Annex 23 study award/report and site schedule, and the finalized 2025–2029 strategic plan or adoption decision. Annex 35 needs a notice, award, or implementation record that explicitly ties `C_DEG_155` to the public-charger proposal. Annex 38 needs the institutional EU TAF/OMVG pre-feasibility report or exact consultant contract.
